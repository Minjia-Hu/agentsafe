# Community conduct

[中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

Treat contributors and users with respect. Critique ideas and code constructively. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, and publishing someone else's private information are not welcome in this project's issues, pull requests, discussions or other project spaces.

Keep examples fictional. Never post private captures, credentials or identifying information to demonstrate a problem.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close discussions or restrict participation. Decisions should reflect the behavior and its impact, not a person's background or technical experience.

To request a private channel for a conduct concern, open an issue titled **Private conduct contact requested**, without names or incident details. The maintainer will arrange a private channel; response times are not guaranteed. For vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
